MantraSangraha – Privacy Policy

App: MantraSangraha (package com.vrishalilabs.mantrasangraha) · Developer: Vrishali Labs, Bengaluru, India · Effective date: 23 October 2026

In short: MantraSangraha does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. It needs no account, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To give you your favourites, reading history and statistics, the app saves the following only on your own device, in the app's private storage:

  • your settings (language, script, theme, text size, home-screen layout, counter preferences);
  • your favourite texts and their order;
  • your reading sessions (which text, when, for how long, how many repetitions) and statistics calculated from them;
  • texts you add yourself under "My texts";
  • copies of requests or corrections you have chosen to send us.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to send or export it (see below). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Information you choose to send us

When you use "Request new content", "Report a mistake" or "Publish for everyone", the app prepares a message and opens your own e-mail app (or the share sheet) addressed to support@vrishalilabs.com. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app. We receive only what you send: your e-mail address and the message, including any text you chose to include. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app's content. If you send us a text to publish, we may add it to a future version of the app; we will not publish your name or e-mail address with it. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file with your settings, favourites and history and hands it to the share sheet, so that you can save it wherever you choose. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Permissions

  • Internet: required by the app framework to load the app's own bundled pages. The app does not contact any server.
  • Vibration: for the gentle tap feedback on the japa counter.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. When you send us an e-mail, your e-mail provider's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
