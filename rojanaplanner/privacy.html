Rojana Planner – Privacy Policy

App: Rojana Planner (package com.vrishalilabs.rojanaplanner) · Developer: Vrishali Labs, Bengaluru, India · Effective date: 31 October 2026

In short: Rojana Planner does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data. It needs no account, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking, and does not even request internet access. Everything you write stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage:

  • your planner entries: year, quarter, month and week notes, daily schedules, top priorities and whether they are done, daily notes, reflections and daily logs;
  • the optional journal entries you choose to create (title, mood and text) and their dates;
  • the 105 notes of each year's notes index;
  • your settings (theme, schedule hours, which years are listed, whether the journal is offered).

The app has no servers and no internet permission, so this information cannot leave your device through the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings → Backup & restore. The file is saved where you choose using Android's file picker, and you decide where it goes from there; we never receive it. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include the planner database in your personal Google backup, which is protected by your Google account and handled under Google's privacy policy. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Contacting support

If you tap the support e-mail address in the app, your own e-mail app opens. We only receive what you choose to send: your e-mail address and your message.

Permissions

Rojana Planner requests no special permissions — no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services, and shares no data with anyone.

Children

The app is a general-audience planner. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
